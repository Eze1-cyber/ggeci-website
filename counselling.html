What to Expect

Our counselling sessions are non-judgmental, Christ-centered, and deeply rooted in biblical wisdom. Whether you are dealing with marital strains, spiritual warfare, emotional depression, or career dilemmas, God's Word has the solution.

  • ✓ Scriptural Direction: Clear guidance derived from God's Word
  • ✓ Prophetic Intercession: Targeted prayer with anointing oil where needed
  • ✓ Practical Steps: Realistic, godly action plans to overcome challenges
  • ✓ Follow-up Care: Continued pastoral support as you walk in victory
