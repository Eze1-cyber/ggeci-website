Our Divine Mandate

Built Upon God's Great Grace

Greater Grace Embassy Church International exists to raise champions of faith, restore hope, and demonstrate the supernatural power of God in everyday life. Our manadate is to see jesus and his finished perefct work on the cross, and to make him known through the preaching of the gospel.

Supernatural Grace

Rooted in the finished work of Christ. We proclaim the liberating truth that divine favor is not earned by works, but received through faith.

The Word

Uncompromising expository teaching of scriptures that builds strong faith, destroys ignorance, and equips believers for practical dominion.

Power & Deliverance

Experiencing the manifest power of the Holy Spirit to break generational chains, heal sicknesses, and establish victory over oppression.

Loving Community

A vibrant spiritual family where everyone belongs, lives are cared for, and believers support one another in love, honor, and accountability.
